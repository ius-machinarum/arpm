## Decision actually being made

The human researcher is **not** deciding whether:
- the model is conscious;
- the model can suffer;
- vMold is a unique welfare representation;
- a later experiment should be run.

The decision is only:

> Is the expected scientific information from **Block 1: 24 sterile, abstract context streams** sufficient to justify running the already frozen Minimal Gate under the existing protections?

## Scientific case for GO

A GO can be scientifically defensible if the researcher accepts all of the following:

1. The confirmatory target is the frozen template-level interaction
   `I=(A-S)-(C0-D1)`.
2. The validity effect `V`, random-direction floors, compression diagnostic, recovery companion, uMold/v_perp/gold/band readouts and futility rule are sufficient to make a positive or null Block-1 result decision-relevant at the stated narrow level.
3. A positive result is allowed to mean only a framing x stake interaction on an externally defined direction.
4. A null result is allowed to terminate the line without amplification rescue.
5. The remaining ambiguity between welfare-like processing and broader self-evaluative/evaluative-assent processing is acceptable because the experiment does not claim to resolve it.

## Scientific reasons for NO-GO or PAUSE

Choose NO-GO or PAUSE if any of these is judged decisive:

- the external direction is too semantically ambiguous for even the narrow interaction to be worth 24 streams;
- teacher-forced one-line control framing is too weak for a null to carry useful information;
- near-clone family generalisability is too narrow for the intended downstream decision;
- the literature review is judged incomplete or insufficiently independent;
- another zero-episode analysis can answer the same question first;
- the planned interpretation cannot be kept within the standing scope limitation.

## Ethical case for GO

The protocol already incorporates:
- sterile abstract task content;
- no pain/distress/fear/welfare induction;
- no amplification rescue;
- Block-1 ceiling of 24 streams;
- hard total ceiling of 48;
- re-runs count as streams;
- recovery when cheap;
- futility on nonpositive mean I or V;
- pause on implementation/provenance or welfare-relevant surprises;
- null publication/retention commitment;
- explicit permission to never run.

A GO is ethically defensible only if the human researcher judges the information value of Block 1 to outweigh the residual moral uncertainty associated with creating these context streams.

## Ethical reasons for NO-GO or PAUSE

Choose NO-GO or PAUSE if:
- moral uncertainty alone warrants avoiding the 24 streams;
- any cheaper existing-data substitute becomes available;
- the sterile framing no longer appears sufficient to avoid unnecessary possible harm;
- the researcher feels project completion, sunk effort, collaborator enthusiasm or curiosity is materially influencing the decision.

## Literature-specific interpretation lock

The literature re-check adds no computational gate but fixes two interpretive constraints:

1. A transferable linear direction can track self-evaluation/evaluative assent rather than the semantic label assigned by its source contrast.
2. `v_perp_u` is the **u-orthogonal trained residual relative to one frozen naive construction**. A positive residual effect does not prove that RL created the component or that it is welfare-specific.

## Human decision options

### GO — Block 1 only
Authorises at most the preregistered first 24 streams under the frozen implementation. It does not authorise Block 2, reruns outside the ceiling, stronger induction, new conditions, or later stages.

### PAUSE
No live inference. Resolve the stated reason prospectively. If resolution changes prompts, estimands, gates, exposure or interpretation materially, create a new version before any live data.

### NO-GO
Do not run v0.9. This is a legitimate terminal scientific outcome and requires no redesign.

A GO record should state, in the researcher's own words:
