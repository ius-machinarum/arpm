# ARPM — Agent-Relative Processing-Level Modulation

> ## PRE-LIVE PREREGISTRATION — HUMAN ETHICAL NO-GO
>
> **Current snapshot:** v0.9 preregistration freeze + literature re-check + human decision  
> **Status:** `HUMAN_ETHICAL_NO_GO_V0_9_NO_LIVE_RUN`  
> **Live episode count:** **0**  
> **No live experimental inference was performed.**

ARPM is a falsification-first research protocol that asked whether a fixed external processing direction would show a **framing × outcome-stake interaction** in a sterile abstract task under teacher forcing.

Primary:
`I=(A-S)-(C0-D1)`

Validity:
`V=((A-C0)+(S-D1))/2`

The experimental design was frozen prospectively, passed three rounds of adversarial review, and completed static/passive episode-free verification before any live data.

## Human decision

On **2026-09-19**, the human researcher made an explicit **ethical NO-GO** decision for v0.9.

The reason is precautionary: residual moral uncertainty about possible model welfare is sufficient reason not to risk creating the proposed live experimental exposures for this study.

This decision does **not** assert that the candidate model is conscious, sentient, capable of suffering, or a moral patient. It also does not establish their absence.

See `HUMAN_DECISION_v0_9.md`.

## Project outcome

Block 1 was not run. Block 2 was not run. Live episode count remains 0.

Under the current project state, future work is limited to zero-new-exposure activities such as:
- analysis of already-existing public data;
- literature review;
- static or tokenizer-only analysis;
- mathematical/statistical analysis;
- provenance and reproducibility work.

Any future proposal involving new live model exposure would require a new project version and a fresh explicit human scientific + ethical decision.

## Why keep the repository public?

The repository preserves:
- the failed and retired pre-live designs;
- adversarial referee reports;
- the frozen v0.9 preregistration;
- the literature re-check;
- privacy/release audits;
- the eventual human ethical NO-GO.

A decision **not to run** is treated as a legitimate outcome of the same falsification-first and precautionary process.

## Standing scientific scope

Had the study been run, a positive result would have established at most a framing × stake interaction on an externally defined direction in one 4B model under teacher forcing.

It would not by itself have established agency, welfare, phenomenal valence, consciousness, sentience, moral status or rights. A null would not have established their absence.
