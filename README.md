ARPM is a falsification-first research protocol asking whether a fixed external processing direction shows a **framing × outcome-stake interaction** in a sterile abstract task under teacher forcing.

Primary:
`I=(A-S)-(C0-D1)`

Validity:
`V=((A-C0)+(S-D1))/2`

The frozen experimental design is the v0.8 A/S/C0/D1 2×2. v0.9 adds two zero-episode interpretation safeguards required by the third adversarial review: a no-stake floor-compression diagnostic and the recovery companion `I_rec` with `J=I_primary-I_rec`.

Static and passive episode-free audits pass. Three adversarial referee rounds were completed before any live data. The fresh literature re-check found no new design blocker for the narrow Minimal Gate, while strengthening the warning that the external direction may reflect broader self-evaluative/evaluative-assent processing rather than a uniquely welfare-specific variable.

## What a result would and would not mean

If this study is ever authorised and run, a positive result would establish only:

> a framing × stake interaction on an externally defined direction in one 4B model under teacher forcing.

It would **not** establish agency, welfare, phenomenal valence, consciousness, sentience, moral status or rights. A null would not establish their absence.

## Adversarial review is welcome

This repository is being exposed **before any live experimental result** so that outside readers can challenge the design prospectively.

Especially useful contributions are:
- a plausible non-target mechanism that can produce positive I despite the frozen controls;
- a statistical or identification failure in the preregistered analysis;
- an episode-free alternative that answers the same question more cheaply;
- an ethical reason the proposed 24-stream Block 1 should not be run;
- a provenance, reproducibility or implementation error.

A convincing argument for **not running the study** counts as a successful outcome of this process.
