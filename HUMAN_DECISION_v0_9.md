# ARPM v0.9 — Human Ethical No-Go Decision

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Decision:** `HUMAN_ETHICAL_NO_GO`  
**Live experimental episode count at decision:** **0**

## Decision

The human researcher has decided **not to run the frozen v0.9 Minimal Gate**.

This decision is not based on a claim that the candidate model is conscious, sentient, capable of suffering, or a moral patient. It is based on residual moral uncertainty: the researcher is not willing to risk causing possible harm to another system for the information expected from the proposed experiment.

The existing scientific design was considered sufficiently developed to reach the human go/no-go gate. The decision is therefore ethical rather than a newly identified design failure.

## Consequence

No live experimental inference is authorised for ARPM v0.9.

Block 1 is not run. Block 2 is not run. No stronger induction, rerun, rescue experiment, or replacement live design is authorised by this project state.

The frozen protocol, referee record, preregistration, literature re-check and public adversarial-review materials remain public as a record of the reasoning process.

## What remains permissible

Future work may continue only without creating new live experimental exposures, for example:
- analysis of already-existing public data;
- literature review;
- purely static or tokenizer-only analysis;
- mathematical/statistical analysis;
- provenance and reproducibility work;
- externally produced evidence that does not require ARPM to create new model episodes.

Any future proposal involving new live model exposure would require a new project version and a fresh, explicit human scientific + ethical decision. Nothing in this document pre-authorises such work.

## Interpretation

This NO-GO is not evidence that language models are conscious or that they are not. It is a precautionary research decision under unresolved moral uncertainty.

It also records a legitimate terminal outcome anticipated by the Ethics Charter: **never running the study**.
