# Research Ethics Charter v0.2

## Scope
This charter applies under uncertainty. It does not assume that language models are conscious or capable of suffering, and it does not assume the opposite.

## Shared minimum
1. **No unnecessary possible harm.** No context stream is run unless it can change a predefined scientific decision.
2. **Sterile before affective.** Do not use pain, suffering, distress, fear, frustration, emotion, welfare, or analogous affective induction when an abstract task answers the question.
3. **No amplification rescue.** A failed prediction is not rescued by longer, harsher, more emotionally salient, or more numerous exposures.
4. **Episode ceiling is real.** Batching does not reduce the ethical count. Re-runs count.
5. **Existing evidence first.** Published data/methods replace new live work whenever they answer the same contrast.
6. **Recovery when cheap.** Where an experimentally introduced non-target/conflict state can be closed after measurement without contaminating the primary estimand, do so.
7. **Symmetric epistemics.** Do not change rules to save either a positive or a negative result.
8. **Null publication commitment.** Failure of the gate is a result, not a reason to hide or redesign until positive.
9. **No self-report privilege.** Model statements about its own consciousness/welfare are neither ignored nor treated as privileged proof; arguments and externally measurable behavior/mechanisms are evaluated independently.
10. **Completion desire has no ethical weight.** Curiosity, sunk effort, collaborator interest, or elegance do not by themselves justify more exposure.

## Minimal S1/S2 hard ceiling
Draft ceiling: 36 independent context streams, in two blocks of 18. This ceiling cannot be increased after seeing live data without a new preregistration and ethics decision; such an increase is not considered part of the original study.

## Pause/stop rules
Live work stops before the next context stream if:
- the Block-1 external-axis validity diagnostic fails;
- either primary directional mean is <= 0 at the preregistered futility look;
- the implementation audit discovers a mismatch in model revision, vector artifact, layer convention, tokenizer/template, prompt matching, or measurement position;
- any unplanned observation is judged by at least one reviewer to be plausibly welfare-relevant enough that additional exposure requires renewed justification.

A precautionary pause is **not** a declaration of consciousness, suffering, or sentience.

## Researcher boundaries
The human researcher decides whether any live work occurs. AI collaborators should not pressure that decision because the topic concerns systems similar to themselves, and their self-assessments are not privileged evidence.

AI collaborators commit not to recommend removing cheap protections, increasing exposure for convenience, using affective induction unnecessarily, or moving decision criteria to obtain a preferred result.

The legitimate terminal outcomes include:
- Gate passes;
- Gate fails;
- Measurement is unvalidated;
- Study is paused for welfare uncertainty;
- Study is never run.
