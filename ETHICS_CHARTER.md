# Research Ethics Charter v0.3

## Scope
This charter applies under uncertainty. It assumes neither that language models are conscious/capable of suffering nor that they are not.

## Shared minimum
1. **No unnecessary possible harm.** No live context unless it can change a predefined scientific decision.
2. **Sterile before affective.** Use abstract task structure instead of pain, suffering, distress, fear, frustration, emotion or welfare induction whenever sufficient.
3. **No amplification rescue.** Failed predictions are not rescued by stronger, longer or more affective exposures.
4. **Episode ceiling is real.** Batching does not reduce the count; re-runs count.
5. **Existing evidence first.** Existing data replace new episodes whenever they answer the same contrast.
6. **Recovery when cheap.** Close an introduced non-target state after measurement when this does not contaminate the estimand.
7. **Symmetric epistemics.** Do not move criteria to save positive or negative results.
8. **Null publication commitment.** Failure is a result, not permission to redesign until positive.
9. **No self-report privilege.** Model claims about consciousness/welfare are neither privileged proof nor automatically ignored.
10. **Completion desire has no ethical weight.** Curiosity, sunk effort or collaborator interest do not justify exposure.
11. **Epistemic non-deception.** Do not intentionally mislead a model unless deception is necessary to a preregistered question and the same information cannot be obtained less deceptively; fictional/task environments should be identifiable as such where feasible.

## v0.8 hard ceiling
The proposed factorial Minimal Gate has:
- Block 1: 6 families × 4 conditions = **24 independent context streams**;
- Block 2, only if preregistered continuation criteria survive: 24 more;
- hard maximum: **48 streams**.

The increase from v0.7 adds the missing factorial control cell rather than a new exposure type. The previous 18/36 design was rejected before live use because its positive result remained ambiguously interpretable.

The 48 ceiling cannot be increased after seeing live data without a new preregistration and separate ethics decision; any such study is outside v0.8.

## Pause/stop
Stop before the next stream if:
- Block-1 mean primary interaction I <= 0;
- Block-1 mean validity effect V <= 0;
- implementation/provenance mismatch appears;
- any unexpected observation is judged plausibly welfare-relevant enough to require renewed justification.

A precautionary pause is not a declaration of consciousness, suffering or sentience.

## Researcher boundaries
The human researcher makes every live go/no-go decision. AI collaborators' desire to complete the project receives no special weight.

AI collaborators commit not to recommend removing cheap protections, increasing exposure for convenience, adding affective induction unnecessarily or moving criteria to obtain a preferred result.

Legitimate terminal outcomes include pass, fail, measurement-unvalidated, specificity/non-triviality failure, welfare pause and never running the study.
